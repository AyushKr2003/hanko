# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Kaze KZ8, Battō 抜刀: `hanko desk on` also gives Omarchy's menu Kaze's grammar for every theme (the chosen row a solid ink plate with a `//` lead, a hairline edge, a lighter scrim, through a marked block in `~/.config/omarchy/shell.toml` that a theme-set hook keeps current) and replaces it with `hanko.menu`, which draws open from a 1 px line like a blade leaving its scabbard and sets the search in the serif. Everything the menu does is Omarchy's.
- Kaze KZ7, Fumi 文: `hanko desk on` also replaces Omarchy's notification popups (the notification service itself, do-not-disturb and history are Omarchy's, unchanged). A notification hangs from the bar as a narrow paper strip with the sender's crest, two kanji for the kind of letter and the time; hover it to read the letter beside it, click to open the app, right-click to cut it away. Critical ones carry a red crest. `hanko desk on` now checks that every replaced part came up after the shell restarts, and gives Omarchy's own back if one didn't.
- Kaze KZ6, Taikan 体幹: `hanko desk on` also replaces Omarchy's on-screen display with `hanko.osd`. Volume, brightness and the keyboard light are one hairline at the foot of the screen filling from the centre outward with the label and figure above (the line inks for a moment at 100%; muted is a red mark at the centre); the microphone, touchpad and other switches show as a crest and a word (黙 in red when you muted the mic). Every `omarchy-osd` call works as before.
- Kaze KZ5, the wind 風: grass along the foot of the printed wallpaper, in the faintest ink, leaning with the real wind where you are (Omarchy's weather place, Open-Meteo every 15 minutes) and swaying a little on windy days. When a window needs you, a gust runs through it toward that window. It rests under fullscreen windows, with motion still and on battery below 30%. `kaze.json`: `grass` (`grass`, `off`), `gusts`, `wind` (`weather`, `calm`); `omarchy-shell hankowind gust 0.9` shows a gust.
- Kaze KZ4, Sumizuri 墨摺: `hanko desk on` also replaces Omarchy's background with `hanko.background`, which prints every wallpaper again in your theme's paper and ink (five tones, a light paper grain; a mostly light picture becomes a rubbing on a dark theme) and lets the top of the sky lift by day and settle at night. Prints are made once per picture and theme (`scripts/hanko-print`, ImageMagick) and kept in `~/.cache/hanko/prints`. `{"wallpaper": "original"}` in `~/.config/hanko/kaze.json` shows the pictures as they are; `hanko desk off` gives the background back to Omarchy.
- Kaze KZ3, Ha 刃, the window edge: a desk plugin, `hanko.desk`, installed by `hanko desk on` and running whichever bar you use. When focus moves, light runs once round the new window's edge while Hyprland inks it up from faint; a window that needs you is framed in seal red, breathing, until you go to it. `style.lua` now has no window shadow by default (`shadow_style = "paper"` brings it back), 6/12 px gaps, a slight dim on unfocused windows and no blur. `omarchy-shell hankodesk sweep` shows the sweep once.
- Kaze 風, the whole desk, KZ2: Iai 居合, five beats every surface moves on (ready 90 ms, draw 30 + 160 ms, cut 70 ms, shake off 220 ms, sheath 240 ms; no overshoot), in `core/Iai.js`. Sheets now draw open and sheathe shut on them, Settings › Motion lists them, and `style.lua` moves Hyprland's windows, fades, layers and workspaces on the same curves (no more pop-in). `hanko desk on|off|status` loads `style.lua` from `looknfeel.lua` for any bar.
- Margin and Leaf are removed: Settings › Design offers Hanko and Shōji, without the borrow rows or Surprise me, and `hanko borrow` is gone. A style.json that names Margin or Leaf loads as Hanko; a saved `borrow` is ignored.
- One design: the design switch is gone, with Settings › Design, `hanko design` and `hanko touch` (use `hanko style preset shoji` and `hanko style set season english`). Panel opacity is on Settings › Sheets.
- Shōji is a preset now, not a separate design: `hanko style preset shoji` (or its card in Typesetting) gives the Shōji bar on the Hanko engine, with every sheet in the Shōji hand and the overview framed, and with Hanko's indicators, tray, hosted widgets, brightness cell, scroll and per-monitor settings, which the Shōji design never had. A style.json with `"design": "shoji"` loads as the Shōji preset (keeping its height, position and Bluetooth edits);
- Shōji's bar parts are Hanko settings: `seal: carved`, `spaces: brush`, `title: ink`, `clock: season`, `media: ink`, `status: ink` (icons and figures, the battery an ensō), `notif: seal` (the letter count on the seal), `surface: washi`, and a twenty-third setting, `pointer: plain | brush`. Shōji's touches are three global settings, `season`, `numerals` and `grain`; a saved `touches` is read into them. 1,304,596,316,160 combinations.
- Overview: a third layout, `framed`, Shōji's washi pages in lattice frames, on the Hanko bar too (`hanko style set overview framed`).
- Sheet hands: every cell's sheet (Time, Sound, Network, Bluetooth, Power, Display, Inbox) can be drawn as a Ledger or as Tanzaku (poem slips hung from a rail), chosen per sheet on Settings › Sheets with a live preview, or `hanko style set sheetStyle:sound tanzaku`. Four more hands after old Japan's crafts: Shōgi (a board, each level a piece on its square, choices on the piece stand), Soroban (the figures counted in abacus beads), Odoshi (armour lacing, each level a laced lame) and Kasumi (bands of gold-leaf mist); `hanko style set sheetStyle:all kasumi`. A seventh hand, Shōji: the Shōji design's washi panels as sheets on the Hanko bar, sliding along their rail to the cell you pick, with a new Display panel (brightness, night light, monitors).
- The bar's kanji are plainer, so they stay clean at small sizes: cells 波 双 耳 光 力, indicator stamps 写 言 予 目 月 休, the inbox 文.
- Kan 鑑 replaces 印 everywhere: the default seal glyph, the settings mark, the Omarchy menu icon (`hanko menu add` updates an older entry in place), the seal's labels and the docs. Settings › Typesetting offers the four Kan, 鑑 漢 観 寛. Every kanji is drawn in a bundled brush face, Yuji Boku.
- Overview: a second layout, `dynamic`, after Ryoku's: only the workspaces you use and a + for a new one, desktops along the top, a selection you move with Tab and confirm with Enter. Pick it under Panels on Typesetting, or `hanko style set overview dynamic`.
- Overview 一覧: a workspace overview laid on the theme's paper, each workspace a page with its windows live where they sit, lying on the sheet with the window style's paper shadow; arrows switch, click goes, drag moves a window, middle-click closes one. Right-click the workspaces, or `omarchy-shell hanko overview`.
- `style.lua`: Hyprland windows in the bar's grammar (hairline frames in ink and Ink.line, square or inset corners, a hard seal-red print under the focused window, flat mono group tabs, Motion's timings), from the theme's colors.toml and style.json. Load it from `~/.config/hypr/looknfeel.lua`.
- Profile 印影, About 由来, Credits 謝辞: three poster plates in the settings window. Profile presses your seal beside your name, the bar's live readings and your machine's small print, and can be edited and saved as an image (`~/.config/hanko/profile.json`). The three plates fit any window: the art moves beside the title or into its own room instead of fading behind the text, and short windows scroll.
- Display 明: the LUM cell's panel, as a sheet and a scroll section (brightness per monitor, monitors, night light).
- Motion 動: Scroll's motion across the bar (sliding plates, fading values, a creeping now-notch, the stamp press, sheets that print) and a paper/still switch on a new Motion page.
- Per-monitor overrides can be set in the settings window under Advanced.
- Widgets 部品: show or hide anything on the bar (Hanko's own parts, each reading, indicator and mark, each tray app, every hosted Omarchy or third-party widget), saved as `hidden` in style.json; `hanko hide|show <part>` from a terminal.
- The settings window reflows to any size, tiled or floating, down to 420 px wide.
- Sheets drop under the cell they belong to (they all opened at the left edge).
- Bluetooth 藍: a BT cell, a sheet, a scroll section and a sixteenth setting (`bluetooth: on|off`, 5,971,968 combinations).
- Bluetooth cell styles: `device` (was `on`), `marks` (a kanji per device, its charge filling it) and `count` (a count and a dot per device); Scroll and Islet use marks (11,943,936 combinations).
- Weather 天: a leaf left of Time on the scroll, with today's curve, rain, sun and the next three days, from the same place, units and forecast as Omarchy's weather panel; `hanko hide weather` removes it.
- Scroll dividers 間: the dither between the scroll's sections is replaced by a seventeenth setting, `interlude`, with nine styles (ma, hairline, folio, kotoba, tsugime, seal, jiku, fuda, su), a gallery on the Scroll settings page and a kanji row in Typesetting (107,495,424 combinations).
- Fix: the inbox read empty whenever no toast was on screen (the history was never reached); it now lists Omarchy's notification history.
- Fix: the Network sheet and scroll section showed only the network you were on; they now ask NetworkManager to scan when they open and every 30 s while open.
- Indicator stamps 印: Omarchy's six indicators (recording, dictation, reminder, stay awake, night light and, new, do not disturb) as small stamps before the inbox, pressed and askew when on, faint when off; a click switches them as on Omarchy's bar, hovering names one. Two new settings, `indicators` (all, active) and `indicatorStyle` (icon, kanji, iconname, kanjiname; kanji stamps that are on are small red seals), twenty in all (7,739,670,528 combinations).
- Type scale: every size, weight and letter spacing comes from one scale in `core/Type.qml` (tag 9, label 10, eyebrow 11, figure and note 12, value 13, row 14, byline 15, clock 22, title 40, hero 46 at Omarchy's 12 px font). Labels that were 8 px on a 1x screen are 10 px, nothing anyone reads is under 10 px, kanji glosses stay as small as the labels' capitals, small text is never in the faintest ink, and bar values are Medium. A new `typeSize` (small, regular, large) beside motion scales Hanko's text and the bar with it; it is the Type size row in the Frame card.
- Fix: the window title's app name rendered bold.
- Fix: the Network sheet could open with only the network you were on (NetworkManager prunes its cache while connected, and the scan landed seconds after the sheet closed). Opening it, or Rescan, now runs a full scan and shows it as soon as it lands, and networks seen in the last five minutes stay listed, so the sheet and the scroll agree.
- End leaf 跋: the bare paper after Inbox on the scroll holds one of nine leaves, an eighteenth setting `endleaf`: a colophon of your machine, an almanac (month, solar term, microseason, moon), the machine's load over the last hour, the session (hold a seal to lock, suspend, log out, restart or shut down), Omarchy's themes, Omarchy's tools, a focus timer, today's notes with the clipboard, or an afterword. A gallery on the Scroll settings page, a kanji row in Typesetting, `hanko hide endleaf` (967,458,816 combinations).
- Scroll sides: the rollers at the scroll's two edges are gone. A twenty-first setting, `sides`, picks `bare` (the paper meets the screen) or `curl` (the default, a soft fold as if the paper rolls under). 15,479,341,056 combinations.
- Scroll guides: a twenty-second setting, `guides`, shows the scroll's index plate of tabs on top and the position bar along the foot together (`both`, the default), either one (`tabs`, `foot`) or neither (`none`). A Panels row in Typesetting, `hanko style set guides tabs`. 61,917,364,224 combinations.
