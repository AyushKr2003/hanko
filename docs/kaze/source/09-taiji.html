Taiji, the standoff

対峙

Tsushima's duels begin in stillness: two figures, wind in the grass, one cut. The lock screen is that moment. Nothing moves but the grass. When you type, a blade draws out under the time and each key is a nick on it. A wrong password is deflected. The right one is a single diagonal cut, and the two halves slide apart onto your desk.
