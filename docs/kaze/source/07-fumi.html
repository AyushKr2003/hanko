Fumi, letters on a branch

A notification doesn't slide in as a rounded card. It hangs from the bar as a tanzaku, the narrow paper strip people tie to a bamboo branch: only the sender's crest and the time, set vertically. It unrolls down from the bar's edge. Hover it, or press Super+N, and it opens sideways into the letter.
