Iai, the drawn sword

居合

Every surface moves in the same five beats, taken from iaidō: ready, draw, cut, shake off, sheath. Opening is quick and stops dead. Closing is slower and ends in a click. Nothing springs, nothing bounces, and with motion off every beat is just its last frame.

Jo-ha-kyū序破急Noh's tempo in one move: a held beat (jo), a fast travel (ha), an abrupt end (kyū). The 30 ms hold before a draw is what makes it feel intended instead of sudden.
Closing is slower納Other desktops close faster than they open. A blade goes back into the scabbard carefully. 240 ms against 160 ms, and the last pixel snaps.
One hand, everywhere一The same tokens drive the bar, the panels, the launcher, the toasts and the lock (core/Iai.qml), and the same curves drive Hyprland's windows (hanko.lua), so everything on screen moves like one person.
