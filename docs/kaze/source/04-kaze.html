Kaze, the wind is real

風

A hundred blades of grass along the foot of the print, in the faintest ink. They lean with the actual wind outside (Hanko already reads the weather for the bar). A still day is still. And when something needs you, one gust runs through them toward it, the way Tsushima's wind shows the road, then the grass settles.

Information, not decoration知Most "Japanese" desktops add falling petals for looks. Here the only moving nature means something: the weather, or a thing that needs you. If neither changed, nothing moves.
Budget節0 fps at rest. Sway is a slow 2 fps nudge on windy days; a gust is 30 fps for 600 ms. Paused under a fullscreen window and on battery below 30%. Blades are one static path set, bent in a vertex shader.
Switches切kaze: grass | edge | off. Gusts: on | off. Wind from weather, or a fixed calm. With motion still, the grass is drawn once at the day's lean and gusts don't run.
