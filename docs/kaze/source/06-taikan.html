In Sekiro, posture matters more than health, and its bar fills from the centre outward. Kaze's volume and brightness do the same: one hairline at the bottom centre of the screen, filling both ways from the middle. At the top the ends meet their caps and the whole line inks for a moment, the posture break: it can't go further. No pill, no blur, no icon.

Why from the centre中Filling from the left makes the line a progress bar. From the centre it reads as balance, like posture: full means "at the limit", not "finished".
