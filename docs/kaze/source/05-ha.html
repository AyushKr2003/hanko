Ha, the edge of the blade

刃

Windows are plain sheets: square corners, a 1 px edge, no shadow, no blur. The focused one has a full-ink edge; the rest keep a fifth of it. When focus moves, the new edge starts faint, light runs once round it like light down a blade, and the edge is left in full ink. That is the only thing Hyprland ever animates on a still desk.
