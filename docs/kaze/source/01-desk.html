01An evening at the desk 夕
The bar is Hanko as you have it. Everything else is Kaze: the print, the grass, the hairline window edges, the focus sweep, and the letter hanging from the bar.
The world speaks first風Nothing on screen says "new message" in a box. A strip hangs from the bar, and the grass leans toward it once. If you don't look, it simply waits.
One edge, one light刃Windows have square corners, 1 px edges and no shadows. Focus is the only thing that moves. Light travels round the new window once in 600 ms, like light along a blade, and then the edge is plain ink.
The print, not a photo摺The wallpaper is your own print mapped into the theme's inks: most of the image becomes paper, the marks become ink. Switch theme and the print is re-inked to match.
