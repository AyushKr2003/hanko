Sumi, ink only

Tsushima's Kurosawa Mode wasn't a black-and-white filter. The team re-tuned the film curve, the lighting and the wind together. Sumi is that for the desk: the whole screen in ink, with a film curve and paper grain, letters held back, the bar stepping away and the wind a little stronger. Only the seal red survives, so "you" and "now" still show.
