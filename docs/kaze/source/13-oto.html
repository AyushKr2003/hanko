Oto, six sounds

Sekiro and the Ghost games are half sound: steel, wood, wind, a bell. A desk must be far quieter. Kaze has six short sounds, all under a quarter of a second except the shutdown bell, all mastered quiet, all off until you turn sound on. Wood and paper for everyday things, metal only for the sheath, a bell only when it matters.
