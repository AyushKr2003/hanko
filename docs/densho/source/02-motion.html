Nagare, ink that moves with you

流

The paper moves as fast as your hand and no faster. While it travels, the ink thins as if it hasn't dried: titles lose weight, rows pale. When it settles the ink soaks back in and the section's seal gives one small press. That is the only overshoot, as everywhere on the desk (Iai: nothing springs, nothing bounces).
