Aburidashi, ink that needs warmth

炙出

Ninja wrote messages in an ink that stays invisible until the paper is warmed. Letters on the scroll do the same: who sent them and when is always readable, but what they say is written pale, so someone behind you sees only that you have mail. Rest the pointer on a letter and the paper warms around it; the words darken into view, and fade again a few seconds after you leave.
