Kaze-michi, the wind's road

風道

There is no index plate to read. When something on the scroll needs you, a gust of fine ink fibres crosses the paper toward it, the same wind that bends the grass on the desk, and then the paper is still again. A quiet scroll has no wind at all. Press the gust (or Tab) and the scroll travels where it was blowing.

ACalm凪Nothing needs you. The paper is still.
BA letter, far away便A new message from Signal. Inbox is wound on the right roller; its line turns red and the gust runs into the roller.
CLow battery, behind you電Open at Display; power falls to 9%. The wind turns and blows back toward the Power spine, which takes a red dot.
Borrowed from the island, made ours借Tsushima replaced its waypoints with wind. Here the wind is Hanko's own (core/Wind.js): the speed and direction the desk already uses, so a gust on the scroll and the grass on the wallpaper are one weather.
Once, then quiet一陣A gust runs for 900 ms when the thing happens and once more each time the scroll opens while it's unread. It never loops. The red line or dot stays until you look.
With motion off止No fibres. The red marks on the spine and the roller are the whole signal, which is already enough to read.
