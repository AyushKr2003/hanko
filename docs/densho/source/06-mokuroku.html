Mokuroku, in the order of a licence

目録

A school's licence scroll always reads the same way: the name of the house, its line of masters, the techniques under poetic names, then the master's signature and seal, and last the student's name. The scroll keeps that order. It opens on the house, closes on the seal, and the way it opens and closes is the desk's Iai: a held beat, one fast cut, a hard stop.
