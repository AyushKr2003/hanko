Fude-iri, written as it arrives

筆入

Nothing fades up. As a section comes toward the middle a dry brush writes its title on, left to right, with a broken kasure edge leading the stroke. The seal and eyebrow come first, the caption follows the brush, and the rows come in one after another in reading order. It follows the paper, not a clock: pan slowly and you watch it being written, pan back and the ink lifts off.

Tied to position位The stroke is a function of where the section is (0 at the spine, 1 in the middle), not a timer. Two scrolls are never the same because two hands are never the same, and reversing simply un-writes.
The brush is the theme's墨The edge comes from the same sumi shader as the desk's print (assets/shaders/sumi.glsl): the theme's ink, a little noise, a few dry streaks. No new colour, no glow.
